## Deadline

## What this is
An interactive registration + games + lead-capture experience for Pedagogy Educational Services' stall at the International Education Show, Expo Centre Sharjah, 11–13 Oct 2026. Runs on 2–3 stall-owned iPads/laptops. Goal: qualified counselling leads for higher-studies/NEET/exam guidance, not just registrations or prize entries.

## Structural vs. visual reference (clarified 2026-09-19 — Abhi asked for this explicitly)
The two old prototypes are references for **different things** — combine them, don't pick one wholesale:
- **`reference/pedagogy-expo.html`** ("the original", built first) — use this for **structure**: the overall step order (question → preference-chip step → game → question → preference-chip step → game → question → preference-chip step → request/finish), and — most importantly — the **country/preference selection mechanism**. Its destinations step is a single screen with a study-direction dropdown plus a `chips()` grid of countries to tap (not swipe), and its courses/exams step is the same tap-chip pattern with built-in mutual-exclusivity handling (e.g. picking "Undecided" or "None yet" clears other selections in that group). **This chip-grid pattern is already almost exactly what the stakeholder asked for when they rejected the swipe-card redesign in `pedagogy-expo-v2.html`** — building from this file's mechanism forward is the right move, not a step backward.
- **`reference/pedagogy-expo-v2.html`** ("the second one") — use this **only** for the brand palette and fonts/typography (per the "Visual design direction" section above). Do not carry forward its swipe-card Path Swipe deck, its CSS-drawn hero illustration, or its page layout — all three are exactly what's being replaced.
- The new build's landing/hero and page layout are being designed fresh (see "Visual design direction"), informed by both: the interaction mechanics and step order come from the original file, the color/type language comes from the second file, and the actual layout/hero is new.

## Standing decisions — do not re-litigate these
- **REVISED 2026-09-23 (Round E), deliberate and explicit, not a silent drift:** Grade 9 and up. 9th/10th graders get a direct-submit shortcut — Register's own primary button reads "Submit" instead of "Continue" once grade resolves to 9th/10th, gated on the same mandatory fields as any other registration, and finalizes immediately with no destinations/exam-prep/quiz/review steps. 11th/12th are unaffected and still get the full flow described below. Business reason: 11th/12th are the real qualified NEET/JEE counselling leads; 9th/10th are still worth a registration record, just not the full experience. See `js/registration.js`'s `isDirectSubmitGrade()` and `session_handoff.md`'s Standing decisions section for the full rationale. ~~Class 10, 11, 12 and equivalents only. No Class 9.~~ (superseded, kept for history)
- English only. No Arabic/other language UI for this launch.
- 2–3 stall-owned devices only, no personal-phone/BYOD path.
- No OTP/real-time contact verification. A record is created directly from entered details; phone numbers are format-checked only. Verification happens naturally when a counsellor calls during follow-up.
- Direct-write-to-backend on submit, no offline-first/local-queue design (small device count on a stall-controlled network).
- Draw odds are equal for every registered, non-duplicate student regardless of quiz score or how much of the experience they completed — this has been explicitly promised to visitors and is a compliance-reviewed decision. Do not build a "complete more, win more" mechanic — **the mechanic stays equal-odds even though the skip-popup copy (below) reads otherwise; that gap is intentional and Abhi-confirmed, not a bug.**
- No score/points shown to the student anywhere in the UI. Completion/correctness can still be logged internally for staff analytics.
- Likely duplicate registrations are flagged for staff review, never silently blocked and never silently allowed to sit as two active untouched entries.
- **Build sequence, confirmed 2026-09-19: everything runs fully local first.** `localStorage` stays the system of record through the Tuesday demo and through full workflow/visualization testing — no external database, no live hosting, in this phase. Only after the complete interaction is built, visualized end-to-end, and passes testing does the project migrate to a real external database and get deployed as the actual live website. Don't jump ahead and wire up Supabase/a hosted DB/a deploy target during this sprint — that migration is explicitly a later, separate step once Abhi says the local build is proven out.

## Visual design direction (resolved 2026-09-19 — Abhi confirmed directly)
- **Keep**: the existing brand palette and fonts/typography from `reference/pedagogy-expo-v2.html`. That part of the old design is right and shouldn't change.
- **Redo from scratch**: the layout and hero treatment. Do not reuse `pedagogy-expo-v2.html`'s CSS-drawn hero illustration (`.orbit`/`.paper`/`.ticket`) or its general page layout — those are exactly what's being replaced.
- **No separate photographic images or stock placeholders.** The only image asset in the whole app is the real Pedagogy logo (`assets/logo/`). Everywhere else, build **interactive, animated visual themes** in CSS/JS (motion, gradients, shape/illustration drawn in code, micro-interactions) instead of photos or illustrations-as-images. This is a deliberate choice, not a placeholder-until-images-arrive situation.
- **Use the "taste" skill.** When working in the VS Code Claude Code terminal on anything visual (the landing hero, the selection grids, the review screen, animation/motion choices), invoke the taste skill to keep the design quality bar high given no images to lean on — everything here is code-drawn, so the visual craft has to come from layout, motion, type, and color, not asset quality.

## Latest stakeholder feedback (this round) — see session_handoff.md for full detail
- Replace the Tinder-style swipe-card interaction (too many clicks, encourages mindless "yes to everything") with a faster selection pattern: show top-10 options as a grid/list to tap, with an "Other" option that opens a searchable dropdown for the full list. **Confirmed 2026-09-19: this grid+search-overlay treatment is for the destinations step only.** Courses and activities stay simple tap-chip multi-selects with no search overlay (their lists are already short) — same pattern as `reference/pedagogy-expo.html` already uses for those two steps.
- **New, added 2026-09-19: after the destination(s) are picked, ask "Are you preparing for any competitive exam?" (Yes/No).** If yes, show a chip list of competitive/entrance exams — but only the ones relevant to whichever country/countries the visitor selected as their destination (e.g. picking India shows JEE/NEET/CLAT/CUET/state CETs; picking UK shows UCAT/LNAT/TMUA/ESAT). Data and full mechanism notes in `data/destination_exams.json`. If multiple countries were picked, group the exams by country rather than flattening the list. If "Other" is the only destination, fall back to a free-text field instead of a chip list.
- No skip button anywhere in data-collection/preference steps. Skip is allowed only on the academic quiz questions, and clicking skip there shows a popup with the final approved copy: **"Completing all questions makes your winning chance higher."** (Confirmed final by Abhi 2026-09-19 — see session_handoff.md Section 4, item 1 for the compliance note: the actual draw mechanic stays equal-odds regardless of completion; this popup text is intentionally more encouraging than the mechanic technically guarantees, a decision Abhi made with that gap explained to him directly.) **SUPERSEDED 2026-09-23 (Round E): both mini-games were removed from the flow entirely** (`js/games.js` deleted, Abhi's direct instruction) — the academic-question Skip above is once again the *only* skip mechanism anywhere in the app, not one of two. ~~Revised 2026-09-21 — games are now a second, distinct exception: each mini-game grants a small capped number of chances (2) before revealing its own plain "Skip this puzzle" button, since the previous "no free skip, must solve it" rule for games meant an unbounded retry loop with no way out (flagged live: "Look again" in Pattern Recall was an infinitely-repeatable loophole). This game Skip carries no popup and no special copy — it is not the same mechanism as the quiz Skip above, which keeps its own always-available button and locked compliance copy unchanged. See session_handoff.md Section 4 item 7 for the full rationale.~~ (superseded, kept for history)
- No per-question right/wrong reveal. Let the visitor move back and forth freely through the whole experience, then show one final review screen with everything they picked before they hit Submit.
- Registration: collect date of birth (not an age band), a real Terms & Conditions link/modal, and two separate checkboxes (T&Cs, and a separate consent-to-contact checkbox that covers WhatsApp/Business-API messaging). Collect both parent/guardian mobile (mandatory — needed to hand over the prize to a minor) and student mobile (optional). If parent number is missing at submit, show a clear, non-alarming popup explaining why it's needed.
- Filter academic questions and any subject-based content by the student's chosen stream/course (e.g., a PCMB student shouldn't see Commerce questions). **Data for this is now delivered — see `data/curriculum_subjects.json`.**
- School/college name field should autocomplete against a real dataset. **Delivered — see `data/schools.json` (518 schools, Sharjah/Dubai/Abu Dhabi, each with a normalized curriculum tag). Wire this in for real, it's no longer a placeholder.** When a school is picked, skip asking curriculum manually and use its curriculum tag (plus `data/curriculum_subjects.json`) to fine-tune question content.
- Use the real Pedagogy logo (`assets/logo/`) throughout, especially a premium "win an iPad" landing/hero treatment — swipe-up banner reveal, optimized first for iPad and Windows laptops. No other images — see "Visual design direction" above.
